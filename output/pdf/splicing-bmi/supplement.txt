## Sensitivity models and annotation resources

The random-effects sensitivity used tau2=max(0,(Q-(k-1))/(sum(w)-sum(w2)/sum(w))) and weights 1/(SE2+tau2), with normal-theory confidence intervals. The model used the identical three-cohort transcript universe as the primary fixed-effect analysis. The retained unfiltered sensitivity used the same three cohorts, avoiding an additional change of cohort scope. GO annotations used the 2026-08-05 release and ontology version 2026-07-26. Reactome used the frozen local snapshot retrieved on 10 October 2026. Complete tested terms, including zero-overlap terms, contributed to each phenotype/collection BH family.

## Genetic source correction and instrument coverage

The incomplete-data irQTL component had coefficient and R-squared labels reversed. The derived MR input corrected 2,433,662 selected rows before final MR; complete-data fields and original exports were unchanged. The mapping was checked across reliable cis rows using agreement of coefficient sign with T and R-squared=T2/(T2+N-32), with a maximum discrepancy of 2.45e-15. No P value, T, N, count of interior ratios, or Hardy-Weinberg P value changed. All 8,179,111 selected source rows were retained. This correction concerns the inherited genetic evidence used here and is not a new analysis in the present manuscript revision.

Cis status followed the supplied flag and agreed with same-chromosome positions within 1 Mb of the supplied transcript start in the audited reliable source. Instrument selection used inclusive reliability, significance, and strength boundaries. Source log10 P values were retained; native probabilities may underflow. LD pruning considered all eligible candidates in deterministic order using candidate-to-retained correlations, without variant subsampling or a candidate cap. Instrument and estimator outputs were checked against dense native calculations in both outcomes. Computational failures were recorded as unestimated, not as null results.

For BMI, 35,470 selected transcripts lacked sufficient strong source candidates, 2,065 lacked matching outcome effects at the source-preparation stage, and 2,119 of the 23,764 prepared transcripts were unestimable after outcome/LD review. The remaining 21,645 had complete estimates. Corresponding obesity counts were 27,899, 1,464, 1,375 of 19,610 prepared, and 18,235 complete. This accounting covers every selected transcript. Lower instrument coverage on X, significance-filtered regional input, and ancestry restriction limit generalization of the genetic follow-up.

## Available MR sensitivities

Among BMI primary FDR hits, multiplicative-random-effects IVW was available for 688 transcripts (442 nominal P<0.05); MR-Egger slope and weighted median were available for 208 (49 and 191 nominal P<0.05, respectively). Among obesity primary hits, the corresponding counts were 29 available random-effects IVW estimates (25 nominal), and 13 available Egger and median estimates (7 and 13 nominal). These selected, nominal comparisons are descriptive. Formal Egger-intercept P values were not present in the native exports. Secondary estimators cannot validate transcripts with only one instrument.

## PBMC comparison and planned analyses

The matched BMI comparison included 220,724 transcripts; mean I2 changed from 31.80% in three whole-blood cohorts to 32.00% after adding JHS. The matched obesity comparison included 219,310 transcripts; mean I2 changed from 27.01% to 28.50%. The comparison conditions on JHS eligibility and does not isolate tissue from cohort differences. Historical partial-region colocalization used a different selection lineage and was not re-audited for the irQTL field-label issue; it supplies no current causal support. Network and mediation specifications and results remain pending.
