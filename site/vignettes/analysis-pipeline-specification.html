Analysis pipeline specification

End-to-end workflow, GWAS QC and rare variants, functional follow-up, AI selection of 3–5 findings, an 8,000-word manuscript budget and implementation gaps.

Scientific workflow specification, updated 2026-10-26. This document improves the proposed end-to-end pseudocode and maps it to the current source build. Requirements describe the intended workflow; they do not imply that every branch is already automatic. Documenting this specification performs no analyses, downloads or model predictions.

OmicsMatic should turn a declared scientific question and available data into reproducible association, replication, functional, genetic and literature results, followed by a manuscript draft. It should accept participant-level data, existing association results or later-stage evidence, and execute the branches supported by those inputs. The rules apply across phenotypes, cohorts, tissues and molecular assays.

Use the study kernel for contracts and provenance, the runnable tutorial for existing commands, and the bioinformatics workflow for source acquisition and genetic inference. This pseudocode is a specification, not a new command or additional accepted study.yaml syntax.

These primary/sensitivity rules are scientific design choices for this workflow. The Cochrane meta-analysis guidance also explains model assumptions and why heterogeneity tests should not select between fixed and random effects.

Dependencies determine execution order. Source/literature discovery can proceed while an analysis waits; MR, colocalization, prediction and networks are conditional branches. A report may document incomplete work, but must identify requested analyses that remain unfinished.

Execution contracts and acceptance criteria

For each planned operation, resolve its exact model, input/result type, prerequisites, eligibility policy, thresholds/comparison operators, testing family, resource budget, outputs and failure disposition before execution. Check actual adapter capabilities rather than treating a registered operation name or a working standalone command as proof of study integration. Missing required quality metadata, unsupported ploidy/models, noncomparable effects or unresolved sample dependence must not silently select a convenient fallback. Independent operations can continue with their own recorded dispositions.

A compatible analysis block is more specific than a phenotype label: declare molecular assay/feature definition, tissue, exposure/outcome orientation, effect units/transformations, model/estimand and ancestry/target-population strategy. Do not pool RNA abundance, isoform ratios and protein abundance because all were tested against BMI, or count multiple tissues from the same participants as independent validation cohorts. Record dependencies across blocks and combine their interpretation in the evidence inventory rather than mixing their effects.

Define separate graph nodes for discovery-only pooling, frozen discovery selection, validation, and all-cohort estimation. Freeze each testing family and exact < versus <= threshold rule; report the evaluated denominator and losses from missing coverage. Association, validation, gene/mask and MR families are distinct contracts. AI impact ranking may change manuscript emphasis or propose an explicitly exploratory amendment, but must not retroactively redefine a primary result, testing family or cohort exclusion to improve the story.

An implementation is conformant only when output manifests and integration tests establish the required behavior, not just command availability. Important acceptance cases include MAC 19 versus 20, preservation of eligible low-MAC set members, unsigned results without beta/SE, multiple discovery cohorts pooled without validation leakage, overlapping cohort rejection/handling, empty/null results, unavailable AI/resources, four/five ranked findings, and an over-budget manuscript. A passing structural test does not establish calibrated inference, successful external services or a validated scientific conclusion.

0. Enter at any analysis stage and complete missing work

Users may supply participant data, association or meta-analysis results, enrichment, networks/modules, risk-score evaluation, MR, colocalization, functional annotations or every completed analysis. A study can mix supplied and native work across hypotheses and stages. An all-results project should support validation, interpretation and manuscript construction without fitting associations, rebuilding networks, repeating MR or downloading new inputs unless those steps are explicitly requested.

Treat each supplied analysis as a typed artifact with a scientific scope, separate from a source cohort. A meta-analysis covering four cohorts is a pooled result, not a fifth cohort. A network edge table is not an association table, and enrichment P values cannot enter a beta/SE harmonizer. Preserve original files and hashes, validate an immutable imported representation, and record externally produced versus natively executed evidence distinctly.

The import contract must identify the artifact/result type; hypothesis and phenotype; feature/variant IDs, build and allele/isoform definitions where applicable; cohort/tissue/ancestry/sample scope and overlap; primary versus sensitivity role; method/model/version/parameters; resource releases; upstream artifact IDs/hashes; complete tested universe, selection/filtering and multiple-testing family; coverage, failures and existing limitations; and the producer/date/citation when available. Explicitly mark missing provenance; schema validity cannot certify calibration, prior QC or biological conclusions.

Complete only uncovered native datasets by default, or use an explicit supplied-only policy. Multiple primary supplied graphs need disjoint comparable scopes; unresolved coverage cannot silently choose a native rerun. See the source-build scope and partial-network guide.

Independent completed native MR, colocalization and fine-mapping pairs can also be reused when the enclosing stage is incomplete. Verify each pair's current source bytes, scientific contract, eligible feature, implementation and all sealed outputs. Retain incomplete attempts, retry their missing work and recompute current stage-wide testing families and coverage; do not copy a previous joint correction or count reuse as replication. An explicit rerun bypasses native pair reuse. Automatic unversioned references require their own source identity before they qualify for pair-level reuse.

An incomplete or conflicting import needs a recorded disposition and a precise repair request; it cannot silently trigger a replacement analysis or be treated as an empty/null result. Rerunning an accepted supplied analysis requires an explicit request or registered sensitivity/amendment. Do not reconstruct FDR from only supplied significant rows, invent a tested denominator, infer absent edges/variants as nulls, or describe unrecoverable participant QC as performed. Missing raw data blocks only analyses that require it; it need not block a report that honestly states the available evidence and its limits.

Native intake, participant QC and association also have scoped resume contracts that defer downstream genetic source files. 1. Study contract and participant data

Define the analysis unit, exposure, outcome, covariates, transformations, interactions, effect scale, reference categories and target population. Specify whether a molecular feature predicts the phenotype or is the response; those coefficients are not interchangeable. A model family such as OLS is insufficient without its formula and design.

Check sample/feature uniqueness and alignment, visits, relatedness, cohort overlap, missingness, impossible values, constant predictors, design rank and preprocessing. Assay-specific checks are required; genotype checks include allele coding, call/imputation quality, frequency, population structure and a justified HWE policy. Use appropriate models for families, repeated measurements and single-cell donors. Report the actual analysis population, exclusions, case/control counts and feature-specific N. Summary-only data require upstream QC records; they cannot establish participant-level consistency retrospectively.

Use histograms/density plots for continuous variables, category counts for categorical variables, and missingness summaries for both. Show raw/modeled scales when relevant. Flag outliers; a histogram alone should not delete participants. Prespecify handling and preserve sensitivity comparisons.

Subgroups need explicit definitions. Comparing BMI between groups defined by a BMI obesity cutoff is circular and does not establish effect modification. Distribution comparisons can be descriptive; an inferential test must match the variable and dependence structure, report effect size/uncertainty and correct the declared comparison family. Assess association differences using an adjusted interaction/contrast. Significance in one subgroup and nonsignificance in another is not itself a significant subgroup difference. Data-driven proposals remain exploratory and do not gate every biologically justified sensitivity analysis.

When the model is missing, preserve the requested error even if literature suggests one. Save the search date, primary sources, suitability, conflicting recommendations and search limitations. A literature/LLM suggestion or software default does not substitute for an explicit scientific model decision.

Treat blank/null models as missing. Use the executable's accepted model names and request review before model-dependent QC when a native association is planned. Preserve standalone descriptive QC under its declared endpoint contract and the separate prerequisites for supplied/configured analyses and unsupported modalities. Preserve conditional requirements for endpoint, assay, dependence and calibration. Recent source queries should incorporate declared public assay and endpoint/design context, retaining a broad phenotype/method search and its bounded date order. Keep retrieval, candidate compatibility and scientific model appraisal distinct; unavailable searches must not authorize fitting. See the missing-model guide.

1a. GWAS QC and association strategy

GWAS requires explicit branches for array genotypes, imputed dosages and sequencing calls. Single-marker scans and rare-variant set tests have different eligibility rules. QC is performed before association, repeated after sample selection where counts or rates change, and retained as a sample/variant flow table. A missing required QC field is an unresolved input requirement, not a silent pass. For imported summaries, record which checks were performed upstream and which cannot be verified locally.

Variant identity and analysis population

Declare genome assembly, reference FASTA/version, assay/caller/imputation panel, chromosome naming, REF/ALT, dosage allele and ploidy. Normalize indels and split multiallelic sites under a documented policy; retain original-to-normalized identities. Resolve duplicates and incompatible alleles before joining cohorts. Strand-ambiguous SNPs need an allele-frequency/reference check or an explicit exclusion from allele-sensitive analyses. LD and functional annotations must match build, alleles and the population being analyzed.

Apply phenotype/covariate eligibility, sample QC and declared ancestry/visit selection first. Compute AC, AN, MAC, MAF, call rate and available case/control counts in the resulting cohort/phenotype analysis sample. For a biallelic site, MAC = min(AC, AN - AC) and MAF = MAC / AN; AN counts observed allele copies under the applicable ploidy. Record hard-call versus dosage-derived quantities, founder/related-sample policy and genotype uncertainty. Do not infer carrier counts from MAC, round dosages to manufacture hard calls, or replace per-variant AN with 2 * total_enrolled_N when observations/ploidy differ. For ancestry or subgroup scans, recompute eligibility in that stratum; a large pooled MAC does not make a sparse cohort estimate trustworthy.

Assess differential missingness and allele-frequency discrepancies by phenotype, ancestry, batch and cohort. Implemented genotype QC boundary

The optional registered analysis.genotype_sample_qc profile now connects an observed diploid common-autosomal panel to participant eligibility, missing-aware KING between-family estimates and unrelated-basis genotype PCA with projection. A separate hard-call source can accompany imputed association data. Initial sample call rates use observed autosomal calls, rather than dosage completeness; the denominator excludes structural sex-chromosome absence. All-pair support, prespecified relatedness actions, source completeness and work limits gate the approved population. Generated PCs enter regressions only when requested. Pre-existing PCs can remain declared covariates without native regeneration. See the sample-QC guide for exact inputs, bounded native execution, held dispositions, source-linked aggregate context and remaining caller/sex/PAR/ancestry/scale limitations. This profile cannot retrospectively validate supplied downstream populations. The external-QC/PCA contract adds exact matrix/sample/hash binding, source-reported pair/support checks, imported scores, per-cohort source choices and explicit native completion of missing PCA. Imported PCs retain their source basis, including a declared external reference, rather than being refitted after phenotype subsetting. Full reuse has separate linear import bounds and performs no native KING/PCA. Source-specific limitations and private provenance stay local; aggregate finding context preserves external origin and the requirement for scientific source review. This does not certify that external QC was scientifically adequate or independently reproduce its computations.

The source implementation now calculates allele number from source ploidy when VCF/BGEN provides it, after phenotype/covariate and participant selection. A dosage-only table without ploidy declares an assumed diploid denominator. The generic association adapter excludes observed non-diploid genotypes and split multiallelic sources with explicit reasons; supporting those inputs requires an appropriate association model. This exclusion does not establish biological sex, pseudoautosomal regions, ancestry, kinship or caller-level quality.

analysis.association.minimum_hwe_p and minimum_missingness_p default to zero (report only). A positive threshold activates an exclusion rule: an unavailable required statistic excludes the variant with an explicit unavailable reason. The HWE implementation uses the conditional exact diploid test on eligible human autosomes. Its declared scope is all samples, cases or controls; binomial/probit study defaults select controls. An optional files.hwe_samples ID list restricts this further to the intersection with analyzed participants. Its hash and source and analyzed counts are retained. Supplying a list does not certify founder, unrelatedness or ancestry status. Dosage-derived genotypes never acquire a hard call HWE P value merely because their values happen to be integers. See Wigginton et al. and the PLINK HWE guidance when prespecifying the sample set and threshold; sample-size scaling, mid-P and specialized sex-chromosome HWE are not implemented by this adapter.

Case/control missingness uses a two-sided Fisher exact test on called versus missing genotypes before mean imputation. Enumeration has an explicit state budget; exceeding it records an unavailable result without substituting an approximate P value. Group MACs count the same cohort-minor allele in both groups, and are not carrier counts or independent stratum-minor counts. No default requirement demands MAC 20 separately in both groups. A declared genotype_kind: hard_calls rejects fractional dosages; imputed data require the declared quality metric, including per-ALT VCF quality where supplied.

The default GLM genotype route uses its documented efficient-score inference. An explicitly registered binary_test: exact-conditional now supports finite conditional hard-call score inference for independent Bernoulli observations with declared stratum intercepts and fixed known offsets. Optional approximate Firth effects remain separate; valid P-only tests survive downstream evidence selection. Resource/numerical failures withhold native BH for the complete family and put official selection into a waiting state. See the exact-test tutorial for its narrow model, import contract and scope. Continuous nuisance covariates, kinship, dosage-based exact inference and broader calibrated routing remain separate requirements.

An explicitly registered binary_test: firth-lrt selects full Firth logit fits and constrained penalized likelihood-ratio inference for independent individual Bernoulli outcomes. This is separate from QC and does not implement saddlepoint or binary mixed-model inference. Method reports and mandatory manuscript caveats retain the estimator and its calibration limits. The REGENIE methods illustrate why sparse and imbalanced binary analyses need an explicitly suitable inferential route. Broader sparse-tail calibration and related-sample routes remain required work. The QC tutorial shows the current configuration and packaged acceptance example.

Explicit Firth logit inference

For independent individual binary GWAS observations, a supplied glm model with family: binomial can register binary_test: firth-lrt. The full Jeffreys-penalized objective is maximized separately for each eligible variant. The coefficient-zero constrained fit retains the same full design and penalty; deleting the genotype column would define a different objective. The test statistic is twice the penalized log-likelihood difference, with an asymptotic chi-square tail on one degree of freedom. See Firth (1993), Heinze and Schemper (2002) and the reference fitter documentation.

No maximum-likelihood null must converge first, so separated nuisance designs can be fitted when the full design remains identifiable. Numerical singularity and nonconvergence stay explicit failures with no association P value. The ordinary Fisher-information standard error is evaluated at the penalized estimate; it is not derived from the LRT P value. Subsequent inverse-variance meta-analysis uses a separate Wald approximation. Preserve both methods and their original testing families. Profile likelihood confidence intervals and population-prevalence correction are not supplied by this route.

The method has no implicit relatedness adjustment. It rejects GRM/pedigree, random-effect/cluster declarations, non-Bernoulli families and ordinary-null conditional score export. The implementation uses portable CPU fitting with bounded blocks and ordered parallel variant fits; production genome-scale throughput is a separate performance question. Study offset_column registers a fixed offset, adds it to participant complete-case QC and prevents accidental use as an estimated covariate. An offset alone does not establish valid LOCO, relatedness or population correction.

Firth estimation does not guarantee nominal finite-sample tails for every rare design. The packaged acceptance audit enumerates an intercept-only fixed-margin null exactly. At N=1,000, five cases and 20 heterozygous carriers, the asymptotic Firth LRT rejects approximately 9.6264% of null datasets at a nominal 5% level. This is an explicit calibration limitation, not a biological finding. Register design-appropriate exact/permutation or other validated inference where needed; do not use point agreement with a reference fitter as proof of tail control. Automatic suitable-method selection, broader calibration and sparse/related set-test routing remain incomplete.

2. Result QC, testing families and comparisons

Audit field semantics as well as headers: beta versus R-squared, SE versus variance/SD, odds ratio versus log odds, effect allele, units, transformations, N and P-value calculation. Model rank and feature-specific degrees of freedom can explain legitimate reconstruction differences. Record numerical underflow; a rounded zero does not imply infinite evidence.

Distinguish invalid estimates from heuristic outliers. Nonfinite betas, nonpositive SEs, invalid P values and failed fits need explicit dispositions. Apply these requirements by result type: unsigned gene/set tests can legitimately have no beta/SE, and cannot enter beta outlier screens, effect scatterplots, direction concordance or inverse-variance pooling as though they did. The current effect-QC policy uses per-dataset median/MAD screening, IQR fallback and an optional unit-specific bound, with defaults robust_z: 100 and min_features: 20. These are conservative audit settings, not biological thresholds. Preserve raw tables and every exclusion/reason; do not tune cutoffs to improve replication. Filtering on estimated effects can alter inference and does not guarantee calibrated FDR merely because the rule does not use P values. Retain an unfiltered sensitivity.

Declare intersection versus available-cohort handling, minimum cohorts per pooled feature and each eligible testing family. An intersection follows QC and can propagate one cohort's exclusion to the common feature set. Missing, unmeasured, excluded and tested-nonsignificant remain distinct. Compute association FDR over the full declared eligible family before hit selection. State whether correction is per phenotype or across phenotypes; per-phenotype adjustment does not control a combined cross-phenotype claim.

Effect comparisons use all matched eligible features before significance selection, with selected-hit overlays identified separately. Save units, matched N, Pearson correlation, sign concordance and optional declared rank correlation. Uncertainty requires a justified dependence model; genes/probes are not necessarily independent bootstrap units. Correlation is diagnostic, not a replication test. A fitted slope cannot justify rescaling incompatible estimates.

3. Discovery, validation and primary meta-analysis

Assign at least one discovery and one validation cohort before inspecting a multi-cohort discovery/validation study. A declared meta-analysis-only design is possible, but cannot claim independent validation. A single cohort can support discovery while replication remains unavailable. Cohort labels alone do not establish independence: account for shared participants, families and reused public datasets using an appropriate covariance model or block unsupported pooling.

Pool multiple discovery cohorts using discovery data alone. Freeze candidates before validation; prespecify per-cohort/combined validation, directional criteria and testing families. Under independence, a declared reduced validation family may use phenotype-specific discovery candidates within a predefined common tested universe. Never select it using validation P values. Report exact 1/2/3/all-cohort membership as appropriate. The all-cohort estimate may subsequently include validation cohorts; its stronger P value is not further independent replication.

Use Venn for small cohort counts and UpSet for larger ones, with exact membership and tested-universe denominators. Hit overlap confounds effect agreement with power and coverage. A cohort with no significant overlap needs investigation, not automatic removal.

Choose fixed/common-effect or random-effects pooling from the estimand and scientific comparability. Record the heterogeneity estimator and interval method; few cohorts make between-cohort variance and prediction intervals uncertain. One remaining cohort is a cohort estimate, not a meta-analysis. Report pooled beta, SE, interval, P, FDR, contributing cohorts/N, per-cohort directions, Cochran Q, I-squared and tau-squared when estimable. Add a prediction interval when justified by the model and available cohorts.

Register leave-one-cohort-out analyses with at least three cohorts, minimum retained cohorts and a heterogeneity-alert rule. Define both a per-feature threshold and the fraction of eligible features constituting widespread heterogeneity. Compare a common feature universe where possible and retain all attempted omissions. Report changes in effects, intervals, direction, significance and heterogeneity. Fewer degrees of freedom and uncertainty can make a smaller heterogeneity statistic misleading. Do not search arbitrary subsets for the minimum I-squared or remove every disagreeing cohort.

Primary exclusions need defensible reasons such as incompatible assays/estimands, duplicate participants or documented technical failure. A post hoc change needs an amendment, the original analysis and an all-cohort sensitivity comparison. The primary result is identified by its prespecified rule and exact artifact, not its rank among sensitivity analyses. Replicated findings are a separate tier.

AI-assisted selection of 3–5 principal findings

AI is used here for comparative scientific interpretation: weigh the impact of the supported findings and propose 3–5 distinct principal findings for the whole manuscript, across phenotypes and analysis branches. This is separate from numerical association testing and transparent statistical candidate tables. It is not a request for three to five findings per cohort, phenotype or method, nor for a five-paragraph Results limit. If fewer than three defensible findings exist, use fewer and record why; never invent findings to fill the quota.

First create a complete finding inventory with stable IDs and exact source artifacts. Group linked SNPs into loci and overlapping molecular/pathway evidence into scientific stories where justified, preserving the underlying rows. Several significant SNPs in one LD block or several annotations of one protein should not become five supposedly distinct discoveries. Distinct conditional signals or genuinely different conclusions may still warrant separate findings, with their relationship explained.

Each candidate dossier contains the hypothesis/estimand, cohort and sample coverage, effect magnitude and interval, adjusted significance/test family, replication and direction, sensitivity/heterogeneity, QC and allele/count flags, fine-mapping uncertainty, applicable MR/colocalization evidence grades, functional/structural predictions, literature support/conflict and missing evidence. Include a meaningful null result, failed replication or limitation when it changes the scientific interpretation. The AI should not see only a top-ten dashboard preview or rank a search-success status as a finding. Apply evidence eligibility before narrative appeal. Retained invalid/unreportable results cannot be promoted to supported positive findings. Major partial-data limitations, weak instruments or an unreplicated result must remain explicit; novelty or an attractive mechanism cannot cancel them. Small P values, database availability and AI confidence are not sufficient evidence of high impact. The shortlist may contain well-labeled exploratory or null findings when those are scientifically consequential, with their weaker/different claim scope intact.

Use two passes: provisional prioritization allocates bounded literature and follow-up effort; final prioritization uses verified results and source appraisal. Reassess any proposed main finding lacking a sufficient literature review within the declared budget, or explicitly qualify what has not been established. Save the ranked list, selected 3–5 finding IDs, supporting/counterevidence, selection reasons, near-miss/rejected alternatives, uncertainty and unresolved disputes. Keep an auditable scientist decision and any override rationale alongside the AI recommendation; review does not require asking for permission at every step of a workflow already authorized by the scientist.

Record model/provider/version, prompts, supplied evidence IDs/hashes, responses, rubric and review decisions through the existing assistance/provenance boundary. Use permitted aggregate evidence; patient-level data are unnecessary for this ranking. If AI is required by the study and unavailable or disallowed by network policy, record ranking as pending or an explicit manual substitution. Do not silently label a smallest-P ordering as AI impact assessment.

Thorough principal-finding discussions

Use Wickenhagen et al., Science 2021 as a depth benchmark; an author-deposited published copy is available. Its OAS1 account connects splice-acceptor variation and isoform prenylation/localization to RNase L-dependent antiviral experiments and patient observations. It also examines null results and limitations, including the relationship between blood measurements, infection-site biology and timing. The standard is an explicit evidence chain and serious discussion of alternatives, not an assertion that a pipeline association reproduces that experimental work.

For each selected story, create a complete finding dossier and a developed discussion addressing:

  1. What this study observed: the exact phenotype, feature/variant/isoform, effect scale/magnitude/interval, population, correction family, replication and registered sensitivity results, with exact artifact links.
  2. What previous work established: relevant primary studies, their designs, experimental systems, populations and specific results, with claim-level citations. Resolve isoform, tissue, ancestry and disease-stage mismatches.
  3. How the proposed mechanism works: explain each link from variant or molecular change to cellular function and phenotype. Label each link as directly demonstrated, supported indirectly, predicted or unresolved; show which evidence comes from this study versus external work.
  4. What could explain the result differently: competing genes/isoforms, confounding, reverse causation, LD, pleiotropy, assay binding artifacts, selection, model sensitivity, null findings and contradictory studies.
  5. What follows and what does not: practical/biological importance with appropriate uncertainty, generalizability and conditions on any clinical or therapeutic interpretation.
  6. What should be done next: concrete experiments or independent analyses that distinguish competing mechanisms, identify required measurements and specify what outcome would strengthen or weaken the interpretation.

Give each story the space its evidence needs, potentially several paragraphs. The main text needs a self-contained synthesis, the strongest evidence, important counterevidence and every caveat that changes the conclusion. A linked finding-specific supplementary note retains the full evidence/claim ledger, detailed literature synthesis, secondary mechanisms, source/method comparisons, full sensitivity results and proposed follow-up. Equal per-finding word allowances are starting allocations, not caps on scientific depth. A large volume of prose, a checklist pass or an AI's self-rating does not certify thoroughness or accuracy; scientific review still checks every substantive claim against the underlying results and primary sources.

Flexible 8,000-word main-text target and unrestricted supplements

Use 8,000 words as an adjustable default main-text target, not a ceiling on the complete scientific account or a requirement to pad prose. A declared strict journal limit takes precedence. An over-target working draft is retained with word counts and an editorial disposition; it is not truncated or rejected solely for exceeding the default. By default the count includes Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion and any Conclusion, with their headings and inline citations; exclude the title page, abstract, reference list, tables, figure legends and supplements. Record separate counts for those items. If the target journal counts them differently, apply its scope and retain the resulting section/total counts. This is a proposed document contract.
