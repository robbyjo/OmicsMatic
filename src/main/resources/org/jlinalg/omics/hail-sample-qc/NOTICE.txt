Native sample-QC metrics follow original Hail0.2.139 (Hail Team, MIT). Original source URLs, revision, wheel and critical member identities are retained in SOURCE-PROFILE.json and the source-intake evidence. Genotype calls, allele strings, missing versus filtered entries and source count/ratio semantics are retained. This component is not a Hail MatrixTable/VDS reader, association model, Batch backend, paper mask or complete Hail integration.
