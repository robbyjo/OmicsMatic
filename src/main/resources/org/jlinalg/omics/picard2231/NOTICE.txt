Native Picard 2.23.1 library-complexity numerical component.

The derived duplication fraction, estimateLibrarySize, f, estimateRoi and
100-point ROI formulas follow the original Picard 2.23.1 DuplicationMetrics.java.

absent estimates and zero-unique-pair behavior are preserved.
