Native quality/length/MAXINFO behavior in ReadQualityTrimmer is adapted from
UsadelLab Trimmomatic v0.41 (GPLv3), by Anthony M. Bolger, Marc Lohse,
Marie E. Bolger, Sebastian Beier, Rainer Schwacke, Bjoern Usadel and contributors.
Source: https://github.com/usadellab/Trimmomatic/tree/v0.41
License: https://github.com/usadellab/Trimmomatic/blob/v0.41/LICENSE
Original publication: https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btu170
Modern versions: https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag331

The native subset retains explicit uppercase-N quality handling, step order,
floating SLIDINGWINDOW thresholds, MAXINFO score/tie/length behavior and
TRAILING's one-base rejection. Interfaces, validation and stream orchestration
are JLinAlg code. Algorithm/reference validation does not imply full tool parity.

Illumina adapter sequences have separate rights and are not included in this
notice's GPL statement or bundled in JLinAlg. Explicit supplied/privately installed
adapter files are used only by the separate original upstream application.
