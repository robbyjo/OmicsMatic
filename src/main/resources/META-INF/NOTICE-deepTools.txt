Native helpers use explicitly supplied signals and normalization parameters.
They do not perform BAM counting/filtering, fragment-length estimation,
blacklist sampling, bigWig I/O, feature matrices, plotting or whole-tool parity.
Missing and non-finite values remain explicitly classified. Additional JLinAlg
