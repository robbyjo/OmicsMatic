The Java WaspRemapAudit filter rules are a modified native port of the selected
original source. They retain its name/coordinate/count and unordered paired-CIGAR
semantics, then independently audit full original-record retention, sorting,
merging, indexed retrieval and complete canonical record populations. A retained
wrong-chromosome coordinate coincidence is explicitly refused rather than silently
repairing original output. Project input/resource checks and reproducibility
controls are separate additions. Original mapping/converter implementations
execute in the user's privately supplied runtime; they are not redistributed.

Synthetic fixture sequences and inputs belong to this project. Fixture expected
results/counters were generated by unmodified original WASP/BWA tools, with source
identities retained. Original program/header path traces are excluded from public
fixtures; scientific record fields remain intact. No author data is included.
