OmicsMatic adapter/native numerical audit copyright (C) 2026 OmicsMatic
contributors, GPL-3.0-or-later. Source: https://github.com/robbyjo/OmicsMatic
The adapter uses private unchanged-source upstream runtimes; no original
FlashPCA source, binary or build dependency is bundled. Native code checks the
complete genotype population, source scaling/PC score/loadings identities,
covariance/eigenvector residuals, orthogonality and trace/PVE. It does not refit
partial PCA, certify global top-k order or assign biological ancestry. Cited
method families do not recover an unreported author patch.
