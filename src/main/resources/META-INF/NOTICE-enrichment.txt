Native GseaStatistics implements fgsea1.38 integer-weight preparation and weighted hit-extremum arithmetic in Java. Original algorithm/software: Alexey Sergushichev and fgsea contributors, MIT; LICENSE-fgsea.txt retained. enrichit0.2.6 running-score conventions and original R4.6.1 phyper/p.adjust supply independent reference checks. Native scores do not estimate permutation/multilevel P-values or NES. Original DOSE/clusterProfiler/fgsea/enrichit runtimes are separate private installations and are not bundled.
