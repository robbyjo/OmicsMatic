FlowJo workspace interoperability profile

FlowJoWorkspace interpretation of sample-local XML, transform mapping, Boolean
references and 128-vertex display-space ellipse conversion was informed by
FlowKit 1.3.2 (Scott White, BSD-3-Clause), commit
f2159043b6a56e527d4baacf97490caf8354618e. Its copyright, conditions and disclaimer
are retained verbatim in META-INF/LICENSE-FlowKit.txt.
https://github.com/whitews/FlowKit/tree/1.3.2

The FlowKit/cytolib/legacy TreeStar biex LUT generator is not copied, ported or
distributed in this Java implementation. CytometryLookupTable is a general
finite monotone linear interpolator over explicit caller-supplied calibration
data. The optional Python helper calls the user's separately installed FlowKit
1.3.2 to export mathematical calibration values; no upstream runtime is bundled.

Existing Stanford Gating-ML transforms retain their separate existing notice.
