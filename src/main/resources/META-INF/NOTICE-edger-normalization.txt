Native edgeR normalization

The Java count-matrix normalization, CPM and RPKM implementation derives from
the edgeR sources by Mark D. Robinson, Gordon K. Smyth, Davis J. McCarthy,
Aaron T. L. Lun and the edgeR team. edgeR is licensed under GPL (>= 2);
the JLinAlg adaptation is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
Original sources: https://bioconductor.org/packages/edgeR/
Qualification and exact source editions: docs/literature/EDGER_NORMALIZATION.md

The split-precision binary logarithm adapts OpenLibm e_log2.c and k_log.h,
v0.8.7, commit 9fbeafcd4f1b6ef6aa3946c1c8faead50f38a94d:
https://github.com/JuliaMath/openlibm/tree/9fbeafcd4f1b6ef6aa3946c1c8faead50f38a94d
