# GSEA catalog integration and source distinctions

The native `broad-gsea-tools` command provides a qualified prepared-data Broad
GSEA subset. This catalog stage reviews 28 ranked-enrichment roles across 250
frozen papers and adds nine missed observations. It preserves all 1,520 earlier
reviews and every unrelated paper/tool use. The generic GSEA family includes
several reported providers; one native Broad integration does not validate all
of their algorithms or complete author workflows.

Read the [global command guide](../vignettes/broad-gsea-tools.md),
[exact workflow scope](../vignettes/gsea-native-workflow.md),
[global qualification](gsea-global-development/README.md) and
[source inputs and licensing](gsea-catalog-sources/README.md).

**Warnings and required actions:** Native Broad execution needs no external
GSEA installation. SKIP3 means the affected analysis is skipped until its named
exact permitted prerequisite is supplied; keep it skipped if installation is
forbidden. Unknown author editions/providers are not mapped silently to native
profiles. FAIL2 means **NO COMPLETE RESULT**: preserve inputs, complete null/model
objects, conditions, partial outputs and diagnostics, correct the named cause
and retry NEW output. Never silently repair identifiers, replace nonfinite
values, change thresholds/editions or swap algorithms. A supervising JVM killed
before final completion status produces no complete result.

## Source roles and limits

The source crosswalk is
`omics-2023-2026/gsea-source-role-coverage.json` after admission. It includes
literal paragraph identities, provider/version witnesses, complete candidate
dispositions and per-role remaining requirements. The audit searches all 250
exact XML sources for GSEA/provider names and broader gene-set-enrichment wording.
Its 56 candidates comprise 28 positive ranked roles and 28 other/unresolved
contexts. A result statement can establish an analysis while leaving its
executor/settings unresolved. Provider capability alone cannot establish use.

- P077 and P220 name Broad 4.3.2 and 4.3.3, respectively. These source profiles
  match the native component, but author inputs/settings remain unrecovered.
  The exact P077 supplement's PDF pages 14-15 describe related MAGMA preparation
  and numerical clusters; they supply no GSEA execution options. P220 reports
  rlog and 10,000 permutations; its null type/metric remain unknown. Use the
  frozen final article, not a same-title preprint.
- P096 names a literal 2023.1 label. All 29 unmodified published author scripts
  are checksum-verified and retained under CC BY 4.0. Script 20 specifies classic
  preranked/meandiv analysis, 1,000 permutations, size 15-200 and a timestamp seed.
  The collection label does not recover the executable edition or historical
  seed. The original ranking files remain required; no explicit RNK export was
  identified by the source audit. Preserve the paper's adjusted-P formula and
  zero/nonfinite boundary. Exact historical execution remains SKIP3 until its
  named prerequisites are supplied.
- P018/P020/P031/P040/P049/P092/P109/P117/P197 use clusterProfiler or related
  providers. P037/P055/P133/P163/P170/P182/P228 name fgsea. P062 names GSEApy;
  P151 names MAGeCK pathway. Their versions, backgrounds, rank statistics,
  one-sided choices and permutation algorithms do not become Broad settings.
- P098's ranked RNA GSEA and GSEApy ChEA hypergeometric operation remain
  separate. The ChEA provider/version does not identify the ranked executor.
  P051's actual tumour-core GSEA is distinct from signature resources and
  AddModuleScore. P042 reports a GSEA result without a recovered invocation.
- Other candidates include ORA, ssGSEA, MAGMA/DEPICT/GSA-MiXeR/PASCAL, resources
  and unresolved enrichment. P032/P089 do not establish a ranking/function;
  P079/P094 need their named supplementary notes. They receive no new GSEA
  positive observation here. P077 supplementary edgeR/voom findings are retained
  for a separate future source transition, without changing earlier family credit.

Native source profiles cover preranked, expression/gene-set and
expression/phenotype permutations with explicit source metrics/randomizers,
weights, meandiv normalization, empirical FDR/FWER, complete null families and
closed RUN/VERIFY/REPLAY. Vendor parsers, expression transforms, mapping/collapse,
collection retrieval, continuous phenotypes, ssGSEA, alternate-provider backends
and complete paper/biological reproduction remain outside this subset.

## Admission and verification

Use the [guarded owner instructions](../../tools/literature/gsea_catalog/ADMISSION.md).
Plan and inspect the exact transition before applying it. The owner executes
the previous edgeR verifier in its exact unchanged admitted checkout, rather
than running an old guard against new metadata. All canonical source/metadata
changes must pass explicit before-image checks and a deterministic post-check.
Failed writes retain new artifacts and restore owned predecessors while
preserving concurrent foreign edits. Old fingerprints are never refreshed.

After admission totals are 250 papers, 2,714 observations, 1,548 reviewed uses,
1,166 pending and 140 scoped integrations (26 native Java and 114 upstream).
Including three existing adapters gives 143. There are 259 missing and 35
method-overlap definitions. Full implementation and paper-wide review remain
unfinished. The standalone proof readers execute no Java/Gradle/scientific model;
they check saved source-bound evidence and do not claim fresh author executions.

**Preserve and separately transfer ignored runtimes, producer JARs and complete
private evidence before changing machines.** One historical exact producer
still requires backup or SKIP3; see the
[preservation record](gsea-workflow-development/PRESERVATION.json). Cell Ranger
remains excluded. Both literature reports remain withheld from Pages. Build
current source; no new release is created by this admission.
