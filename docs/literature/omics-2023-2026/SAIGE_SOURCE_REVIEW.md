P194 uses SAIGE to generate a GRM and then describes a custom greedy unrelated cohort selection after phenotype/covariate complete-case filtering. Its threshold is `2^(-3.5)`;the tie rule and degree-update policy are unstated. The separate native [unrelated-select component](UNRELATED_SELECTION_WORKFLOWS.md) uses explicit ties and initial/static eligible degrees. It does not establish a SAIGE association adapter or identical paper-selected IDs.

Direct cohort fitting,alternative-engine descriptions,published-summary reuse,null-model framework reuse,single-variant tests,gene tests and meta-analysis remain distinct. Source paper options differ substantially:LOCO,sparse GRM thresholds,MAC categories,IRNT,conditional tests,Firth cutoffs and historical versions require separate reconstruction. A burden calculated elsewhere is not automatically a SAIGE gene test.

The authentic original1.5.2 build and controlled binary prototype have bounded scientific checks,described in [runtime status](SAIGE_RUNTIME_STATUS.md). **SAIGE,SAIGE-GENE and Meta-SAIGE remain missing project integrations.** Required action:skip their dependent project analyses until a typed,admitted workflow is validated;do not use native graph selection as a mixed-model substitute. No full-paper or semantic completeness claim.
