# Original source and research-input notices

The project adapter and validation programs carry their own GPL-3.0-or-later
notices. The twelve unchanged original source archives in the logical evidence
tree retain their upstream notices and licenses. Exact archive identities,
original license declarations and whole member inventories are in
`tools/literature/singler_workflows/source-profiles.json`; complete original
archives preserve accompanying license files. No upstream algorithm is patched.

Installed runtimes, package caches and libraries are not bundled. Synthetic
scientific records preserve complete original returned objects and conditions,
including captured function bodies and environments where produced. These are
records of original computations, not replacement package implementations.

Article XMLs and the inspected FDProject author notebook remain research inputs
in the private source cache under their original terms. They are not included
in this bundle or relicensed as project software. The public primary census
retains article links, original license URLs and exact source/paragraph hashes.
Privately retained operational receipts and any native memory dumps are declared
in `PRIVATE-RECORDS.json`; available scientific records and raw analysis consoles
are not withheld by that declaration.
